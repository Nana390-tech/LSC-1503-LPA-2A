Self-Reflection: “About You” Project

Before you start

Think about your “About You” project. You prepared a script and recorded a two-minute audio about yourself.

Answer the questions below in complete sentences. Be honest about your experience — there are no right or wrong answers here. Aim for three to four sentences per question.

1 What did you learn about writing a script for speaking?

Think about how you organised your ideas, connected your sentences, and made your script sound natural.

0 words
Before this project, I thought… I learned that writing for speaking is different because… To connect my ideas, I used words like…
2 What was the easiest part of the project for you? Why?

This could be writing the script, talking about yourself, practising, recording, or something else.

0 words
The easiest part for me was… This was easy because… I did not have any problems when I…
3 What was the most difficult part of the project for you? Why?

Explain any problems you had and how you tried to solve them.

0 words
The most difficult part was… I had a problem with… because… To solve this problem, I…
4 When you listened to your final audio, what did you notice about your speaking?

Think about your pronunciation, fluency, confidence, speed, pauses, or use of English.

0 words
When I listened to my audio, I noticed that… I was surprised that my voice sounded… My pronunciation of … was clear, but … was difficult to hear.
5 If you did this project again, what would you do differently or improve?

Give at least one specific example of something you would change.

0 words
Next time, I would… For example, I would practise… before recording. I would also change… because…
